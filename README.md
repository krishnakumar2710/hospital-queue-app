## 💡 About SwasthyaQ
In government and public healthcare hospitals, patients frequently waste hours standing in physical lines just to obtain a registration token[cite: 21]. Without fixed turn timings, individuals from distant areas lose entire days for a routine check-up[cite: 21]. SwasthyaQ is an intelligent queue management and digital token ecosystem designed to modernize outpatient department (OPD) visits.

---

## 🛑 Defining the Problem
1. **Token Only at the Counter:** Patients must arrive very early in the morning and wait in lengthy physical lines merely to receive a token[cite: 21].
2. **No Fixed Time:** Patients have no visibility into when their turn will actually arrive, leading to overcrowded waiting areas[cite: 21].
3. **Long Travel + Long Wait:** Rural and out-of-town patients lose full productive days for a single brief consultation[cite: 21].

---

## ✨ Key Features Implemented in the Prototype

* **🎟️ Remote Digital Token Generation:** Allows patients to secure their queue position directly from their mobile device, skipping early-morning counter lines.
* **📊 Live Queue Status Tracker:** Displays estimated turn times, department details, and the exact count of patients ahead in real time.
* **🏥 Department Categorization:** Supports multiple hospital departments including General Medicine, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, and Cardiology.
